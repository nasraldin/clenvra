# Issues found in real use

Found by the owner while freeing ~75 GB on the work Mac with Clenvra 1.0.0 (2026-10-28). Each item says whether it is a bug or a design gap, and which redesign slice (`docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-28-ui-redesign-design.md`) fixes it. Bugs get a failing test first.

| # | Where | What happens | What should happen | Kind | Slice | Status |

| F2 | Space Map (storage bar) | After removing big files, the disk bar and free space still show the old numbers | Free space and the bar update right after a cleanup, on every page that shows them | Bug | 3 | Fixed: disks are read again after every cleanup (test); the result explains that space in the Trash is freed when it is emptied, with Open Trash |
| F3 | All pages | Opening a page starts a scan or search automatically | Nothing scans until the person clicks Scan. Pages show what was found last time (with its date) or an empty state with a Scan button | Design | 2, 3, 4, 5 | Fixed: System Junk, Developer Junk, Developer Tools, Space Map, Large & Old Files, Downloads, Leftovers and Extensions wait for Scan. Applications and Startup Items list what is installed, which changes nothing |
